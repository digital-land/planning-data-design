### What are some potential uses of Postcodes data?

#### Searching for an area

Most people know their post code and use it to refer to or specify a geographic area.

It is usually the first thing people use to narrow down an area they are concerned with.

#### Statistics

Post codes can be used to group data together for research and statistical analysis.

### What's the value of adding Postcodes data to the platform?

Making postcodes available through the platform means service builders can use them in their services. This would help their users because it is common for people to know and use post codes to find or refer to an area.
