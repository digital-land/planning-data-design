## Backlog

### Who asked for 'Other sports facilities' data?

Identified by the Geospatial Commission as a part of a national dataset to make data easier to find for the sector.

### What is the driver for the request?

Other

### Which 2024 focus area does the request support?

Modern planning software;Faster local plans

## Screen

### What is the 'Other sports facilities' consideration?

Courts and greens comprising natural or artificial surfaces, including tennis courts, bowling greens, athletics tracks and other outdoor sports areas

### Is there legislation that defines 'Other sports facilities'?

No

### Who, in law, is responsible for the planning consideration or makes decisions about 'Other sports facilities'?

Nothing was found through legislation, but we expect that the LPA is responsible for decision making with regards to other sports facilities as it is a local designation.

### Which organisations do we think should publish the data?

Local authorities;Sport England

### Is the Other sports facilities a trigger?

No

### Is the Other sports facilities something to consult during plan making?

Yes

### Provide information about when Other sports facilities is used during plan making

LPAs are likely to have identified the presence of all sports facilities present in the borough. The presence of these will likely be assessed through an open space study, playing pitch strategy and/or an Infrastructure Delivery Plan, and when planning for growth, new provision will be modelled, and be expected to be delivered through development.

### Is there any Other sports facilities data already available?

Yes

### What Other sports facilities data is currently available?

- [OS Open Greenspace](https://www.ordnancesurvey.co.uk/products/os-open-greenspace#overview) (publisher: Ordnance Survey; coverage: National; licence: OpenData - Open Government Licence; fields: id;function;distinctiveName1;distinctiveName2;distinctiveName3;distinctiveName4;geometry)

### Do we think the data should come from a single source?

Yes, the data already exists nationally and openly.
As per some research summarised in this [blog](https://digital-land.github.io/blog-post/our-approach-to-open-space/), we will use the national data in the interim, but long term would like to collect this data from LPAs.

### Will a data standard be required?

No
