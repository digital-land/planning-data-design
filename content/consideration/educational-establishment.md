## Screen

### What is the 'Educational establishment' consideration?

This dataset includes schools, number of pupils, number of boys, number of girls, number of pupils eligible for free school meals and percentage of children eligible for free school meals.

### Is there legislation that defines 'Educational establishment'?

Yes

### What is the legislation that defines 'Educational establishment'?

Education Act 1944

### Who, in law, is responsible for the planning consideration or makes decisions about 'Educational establishment'?

There is no legislation around publishing educational establishments, but the accountability of them lies with the Department for Education, and the education authority (often county councils).

### Which organisations do we think should publish the data?

Department for Education

### Is the Educational establishment a trigger?

No

### Is the Educational establishment something to consult during plan making?

Yes

### Provide information about when Educational establishment is used during plan making

The presence, distance, and capacity of existing schools are something to be consulted as part of plan-making, during the assessment of sites prior to site allocation, as well as when assessing existing infrastructure capacity when planning for growth. Understanding existing school capacity also allows for education authorities to plan for the need for expansion of existing schools, and the delivery of new schools.

### Is there any Educational establishment data already available?

Yes

### What Educational establishment data is currently available?

- [All establishment data](https://get-information-schools.service.gov.uk/Downloads) (publisher: Department for Education; coverage: UK; licence: OGL3)

### Do we think the data should come from a single source?

Yes. The department for Education make this data available through the [Get Information about Schools service](https://get-information-schools.service.gov.uk/)

### Will a data standard be required?

No

### What are some potential uses of Educational establishment data?

There are policies that exist that require people to know where educational establishments (or schools) are.

For example, since 2017, [London has a policy prohibiting open fast food shops within 400 metres of a school](https://www.london.gov.uk/press-releases/mayoral/mayor-cracks-down-on-new-takeaways-near-schools).
