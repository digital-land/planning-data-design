## Backlog

### Who asked for 'Boundary line' data?

The Geospatial Commission published a list of 65 datasets to help the planning process. Within this list,  Council Wards was recommended, but a national dataset was found through OS which captured council wards alongside other, being "Boundary Lines."

### What is the driver for the request?

Other

### Which 2024 focus area does the request support?

Modern planning software

## Screen

### What is the 'Boundary line' consideration?

Boundary-Line lets you show on a map where the line's being drawn, right down to the level of individual properties.

### Is there legislation that defines 'Boundary line'?

No

### Who, in law, is responsible for the planning consideration or makes decisions about 'Boundary line'?
