## Screen

### What is the 'Central activities zone' consideration?

Central areas of London with implications for planning designated by the Greater London Authority (GLA).

### Is there legislation that defines 'Central activities zone'?

Yes

### What is the legislation that defines 'Central activities zone'?

There is NO legislation defining the central activities zone.

There is [supplementary planning guidance (2016)](https://www.london.gov.uk/sites/default/files/caz_spg_final_v4.pdf) on the CAZ, which states that “the CAZ is characterised by a diverse mix of places and functions and planning for the Zone is distributed across ten boroughs”, with those 10 boroughs being the City of London, City of Westminster, Camden, Hackney, Islington, Kensington & Chelsea, Lambeth, Southwark, Tower Hamlets and Wandsworth.
In the original London Plan, this defines the CAZ as “the area in central and east London where planning policy promotes finance, specialist retail, tourist and cultural uses and activities.”

### What is the specific part of the legislation that describes how a 'Central activities zone' gets designated?

There is no legislation that defines how the CAZ was designated.

### What is the specific part of the legislation that requires the publication of 'Central activities zone'?

There is no legislation which requires the publication of this data.
