## Week beginning 17 Feb

- We have prepared a lean coffee style workshop for the next advisory group session.
- We have created a first draft of the [information models for each component](https://github.com/digital-land/planning-application-data-specification/discussions?discussions_q=is%3Aopen+label%3Acomponent)
