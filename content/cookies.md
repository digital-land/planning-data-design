Cookies are small files saved on your phone, tablet or computer when you visit a website.

## How we use cookies

This website does not set any cookies. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, so you do not need to accept or reject anything.

We do not collect any personal information that could be used to identify you.

## Cookies from the previous version of this website

An earlier version of this website used cookies to remember your cookie choices and, if you agreed, to measure how the site was used with Google Analytics. If your browser still holds any of these, they are no longer used and you can delete them:

| Name | Purpose | Expires |
|---|---|---|
| `cookies_preferences_set` | Recorded that you had seen the cookie message | 1 year |
| `cookies_policy` | Saved your cookie choices | 1 year |
| `_ga`, `_gid`, `_gat` | Google Analytics, counted visitors | Up to 2 years |

## Links to other websites

This website links to other websites, including GitHub, GOV.UK, Google Docs and Google Drive. They have their own cookie policies, which apply when you visit them.

## Manage cookies in your browser

Find out more about [how to manage cookies](https://ico.org.uk/for-the-public/online/cookies/) in your browser.
